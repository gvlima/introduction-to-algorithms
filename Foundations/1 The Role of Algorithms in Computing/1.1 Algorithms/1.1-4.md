How are the shortest-path and traveling-salesman problems given above similar? How are they different?

Similar: Both problems can be modeled by a graph and look for a minimum-cost route over a weighted graph.
Different: The shortest path connects one source vertex to one destination, and can be solved in polynomial time. The traveling salesman must visit every vertex exactly once and return to the start, and it is NP-complete, with no known polynomial-time algorithm.
