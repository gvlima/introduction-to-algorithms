Come up with a real-world problem in which only the best solution will do. Then come up with one in which a solution that is “approximately” the best is good enough.

Only the best will do: a bank computing account balances and interest. The exact figure is required by accounting and legal rules, so an answer that is close to correct is still wrong.
Approximately the best is good enough: a delivery company choosing the order of stops so its trucks travel the shortest total distance.
